🌱 Beginner guide — read this and you are a professional

Let's work this out in a step-by-step way to be sure we have the right answer. No jargon without an explanation. Each step ends with something you can run.

Step 1 — Seals. SHA-256 turns any text into a 64-character seal. Change 30→90, whole seal changes (52% bits).
PYTHONPATH=src python3 experiments/01_fingerprint.py
Step 2 — Notebook. Every page seals the previous page. Rewrite page 1 → seal breaks → page 2 points at stale seal.
PYTHONPATH=src python3 experiments/02_chain_tamper.py
Step 3 — Lottery. Re-sealing is cheap, so each page must win a lottery: try numbers until the seal starts with N zeros (~16× per zero).
PYTHONPATH=src python3 experiments/03_mining_cost.py
Step 4 — Banknotes, not balances. No balances stored. Alice breaks her 50-note: 30→Bob + 20→Alice change.
PYTHONPATH=src python3 experiments/04_double_spend.py
Step 5 — Signatures. Secret number → address by curve multiplication. Sign payment ID; verify with address only.
PYTHONPATH=src python3 experiments/05_signatures.py
Step 6 — Agreement. Everyone holds a full copy; most-work valid fork wins; next block breaks ties.
PYTHONPATH=src python3 experiments/06_forks.py

Checkpoints: after Step 2 you can explain tamper-evidence; after Step 4 the double-spend; after Step 6 the 51% rule. Then prove it in the quiz below.

❓ FAQ (interview answers)

What stops the double-spend?Signatures authorise; the chain orders. Pool reserves the coin (2nd spend rejected at creation), blocks reject duplicate inputs, forks resolve by most work. Try Exps 04 + 06.
Where is my balance?Nowhere on-chain. Nodes store unspent outputs; wallets sum yours. See /address/….
Why 6 confirmations?At 10% attacker: ~0.024% success — but ~13% at 30%. At 50%+: nothing helps. It's economics (hardware + energy + price crash), not cryptography.
Is this real money?No — learning toy. No fees/Merkle/scripts/halving/persistence. That list is the extension roadmap.
What do I break on purpose?Reward 50→5000 (Exp 02), pool double-spend (04), forged amount (05), secret fork (06). Each script shows the rejection.
